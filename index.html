About Me.

Between Design & Code.

I’m interested in what happens when creativity meets technology. I enjoy moving between visual design, development and interactive experiences, rather than staying within one medium.

My projects often begin as an idea, then evolve through experimentation — designing an interface, writing code, building an environment or figuring out how people will interact with it.

I'm particularly interested in creating digital experiences that are not only visually engaging, but functional and enjoyable to use.

PROJECT [ 02 ] — URBAN SPACES

URBAN
SPACES
PROJECT EXECUTION ROLE: LEAD DESIGNER | YEAR: 2024

A minimalist approach to urban dwelling, blending modern architecture with community spaces. This project creates a modular living environment that adapts to human interaction, fostering connection.

Inspired by a magazine-spread aesthetic, the design uses bold typography and asymmetric grid structures to tell the spatial story through clarity, rhythm, and atmosphere.

FORM
STORY
PROJECT EXECUTION ROLE: VISUAL DESIGNER | YEAR: 2025

Form Story explores spatial identity as a narrative device, using layered textures, sculptural geometry and restrained palettes to create immersive environments that feel both tactile and cinematic.

The visual language balances editorial framing with practical storytelling, guiding users through a polished journey shaped by rhythm, view, and material character.

GRID
PROJECT EXECUTION ROLE: UX STRATEGIST | YEAR: 2024

Citizen Grid proposes a responsive community infrastructure that reconnects neighbourhoods through shared public rituals, flexible spaces and intuitive wayfinding.

Structured around clarity and proportion, the concept uses visual pacing and calm typography to frame a future where everyday civic life feels more connected and human-scaled.
